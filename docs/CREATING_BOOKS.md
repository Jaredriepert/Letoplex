# Creating Books

The **Create** tab (next to *My Books*) is a built-in editor for making branching picture books. It works on phones, but is most comfortable on a computer.

## Quick start

1. **Create → New book**.
2. **Add pictures** and pick all your page images at once. Each becomes a page, laid out left to right and linked in order. Titles come from the file names (`03_the-dark-forest.png` → "The dark forest").
3. If the pictures are larger than phones need, you'll be offered **Optimise for phones**. It shrinks them to phone size (about 1080×1920) as WebP, which keeps books small enough to fit on readers' phones. Choose *Keep originals* to skip this.
4. Add branches (see below), then use **Preview** to read the book exactly as readers will.
5. **Export**:
   - **Download .letoplex file**: a single file to share or back up.
   - **Share…** (phones): send it with Messages, email or AirDrop, or save it to Files.
   - **Add to my library**: read it on this device straight away.

Drafts save automatically on the device as you work. **Undo/redo** is in the header (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z on a computer).

## The map

Each page is a tile on a grid. Positions are only for laying out the story; readers never see the grid.

- **Tap a tile** to edit that page. On a computer it opens in a side panel; on a phone it fills the screen.
- **Tap `+`** in an empty square to add a blank page there. If a page is selected and the new one is right next to it, they're linked both ways.
- **Tap the circle between two neighbouring pages** to change how they're linked. Each tap moves to the next state: ⇄ both ways → → one way → ← the other way → not linked.
- **Drag tiles** (computer) or use **Move on the map** in the page panel to rearrange.
- **Select several pages**: on a computer, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on Mac) or Shift-click pages; in the Pages list, Shift-click selects everything between two pages. On a phone, tap **Select**, tap the pages, then **Edit**. Ctrl+A selects every page. With several selected you can move them together (drag any of them, or use the arrows), put them all in a chapter (including a new one), or delete them.
- Badges: **Start** marks the first page, **End** marks pages with no way forward, and **↗** marks a link to a page that isn't next door.
- The coloured stripe on each tile shows its chapter.

## Branching

In the page panel, **Where the reader can go** has one setting per direction:

- Pick any page, *Nothing (blocked)*, or **+ New page** to create a page in that direction, already linked both ways.
- **Add way back** appears when the other page doesn't link back yet.
- Readers swipe or tap toward a direction to follow it. Swiping left moves to the page on the **right**, like turning paper.

## Chapters

In **Book → Chapters** you can add, rename, reorder and delete chapters. In the page panel, choose a page's chapter (or **+ New chapter** to make one on the spot).

Two different "first pages":

- **Make this the book's first page**: where *Start reading* begins the whole book.
- **Make first page of this chapter**: where choosing that chapter on the chapter screen begins.

Readers unlock a chapter when they first reach any page in it.

## Check

The **Check** tab lists anything that would stop the book exporting (pages without pictures, no start page, no title) and things worth a look (pages that can't be reached from the start, empty chapters). Each item links to the page it's about.

## Editing an existing book

From **My Books**, open a book's **⋯ → Edit in Create**. This copies it into a new draft; the library copy isn't touched until you export with **Add to my library** and choose **Replace**. Editing the built-in sample makes your own copy under a new name.

## Tips

- Portrait pictures suit phones best. Keep important details away from the edges if you choose *Fill the screen* in Book settings.
- Give pages short titles; they're shown at the top of the reader.
- The **Description** field is read aloud by screen readers and shown if a picture can't load.
- Drafts live only on this device. Download a `.letoplex` now and then as a backup.
